Past seasons

The Philharmonic was founded in 1939 and has given concerts in Talas every season since, except the spring of 1992 and the summer of 2007. Since 1974 its home is the round hall on Gagarin Street. Here is the record, season by season, as the box office ledgers and the old programmes keep it.

  • The first hall · 1939–1956
    Director: Abdykadyr Toktomushev (1939–1956).
  • Ak-Kuu and the epic · 1957–1973
    Director: Sultan Mamytov (1957–1973).
  • The new hall · 1974–1991
    Director: Jyrgal Bekmuratov (1974–1991). Chief conductor: Vladimir Tsoi (1974–1988), Anvar Kasymaliev (1988–2014).
  • The cold years · 1992–2006
    Director: Asylbek Kurmanov (1992–2006). Chief conductor: Anvar Kasymaliev.
  • After the war · 2007–2019
    Director: Raisa Japarova (2007–2019). Chief conductor: Murat Esenaliev (2003–2014), Bakyt Omurzakov (since 2014).
  • The listed hall · 2020–2026
    Director: Cholpon Satarova (since 2020). Chief conductor: Bakyt Omurzakov.
