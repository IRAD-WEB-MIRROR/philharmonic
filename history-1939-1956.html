The first hall, 1939–1956

Director: Abdykadyr Toktomushev (1939–1956).

The State Philharmonic of the Iradi SSR was formed by order of the republic’s Council of People’s Commissars to give concerts in Talas and send brigades to the districts. Until 1974 it had no hall of its own: concerts were in the Hall of Columns of the House of Trade Unions.

DateWhat happened
1 Nov 1939The State Philharmonic of the Iradi SSR is founded in Talas: a small orchestra, a choir, a lecture bureau and two concert brigades.
21 Dec 1939First concert in the Hall of Columns: Tchaikovsky and Glinka, and Iradi songs arranged for choir.
Jul 1941Concert brigades leave for the hospitals and the call-up camps. In four years they give about 3,000 concerts.
12 Mar 1944The Philharmonic’s choir sings in Kobozev at the opening of the refinery club.
5 Oct 1946The first season after the war opens with Beethoven’s Fifth.
Jun 1951A folk orchestra of komuz and kyl-kyak is formed at the Philharmonic.
9 May 1955Victory Day concert in the open air on Central Square, 6,000 listeners.
