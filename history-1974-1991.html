The new hall, 1974–1991

Director: Jyrgal Bekmuratov (1974–1991). Chief conductor: Vladimir Tsoi (1974–1988), Anvar Kasymaliev (1988–2014).

The round hall, Tündük, opened in 1974 with its own orchestra, the Talas Symphony Orchestra, formed from the radio orchestra and the Conservatory’s teachers. These were the years of the full subscription seasons and the long tours.
