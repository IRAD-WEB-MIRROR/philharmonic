The cold years, 1992–2006

Director: Asylbek Kurmanov (1992–2006). Chief conductor: Anvar Kasymaliev.

The spring of 1992 stopped the concerts for the only time since the war. The orchestra came back in the autumn with four folk instruments on its strength and kept playing through the territorial years, often in coats.
