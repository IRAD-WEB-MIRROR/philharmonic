After the war, 2007–2019

Director: Raisa Japarova (2007–2019). Chief conductor: Murat Esenaliev (2003–2014), Bakyt Omurzakov (since 2014).

The hall closed in the spring of 2007 and reopened in September. The winter of the dam, in 2008 and 2009, put the concerts into daylight. Since 2014 the orchestra has been led by Bakyt Omurzakov.
